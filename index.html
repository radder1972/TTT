Our Mission

The story of bridging cultures and enabling genuine human connection in real-time.

The story of True Time Thai began in the heart of Pattaya, at a place where the hustle and bustle of daily life and the dreams of international visitors intersected. The founders, a close-knit team that wanted to close the gap between the local Thai culture and the rest of the world, saw a growing need: travelers and expats often struggled with the fine nuances of language and time perception in a country that lives according to its own rhythm.

The mission was clear: to create 'true time' through seamless communication. They believed that real connection only arose when language barriers were removed by technology that felt human. The company started in a modest, dated building on a busy street. It was a place that at first glance looked unremarkable, but inside it grew into a technological hub with an atmosphere somewhere between a trendy coffee shop and an innovative service center.

While the tangle of power lines characterized the street scene outside, they worked inside on their core product: AI-driven translation services that could convey not just words, but also emotion and context. The logo of True Time Thai – a refined, flower-like symbol that radiates peace and connectivity – quickly became a well-known sign of reliability and hospitality.

The strength of the company lay in the personal approach. On the walls hung Polaroid photos telling the stories of the people they had helped: couples who learned to understand each other better through their technology, business partners who bridged the gap, and travelers who could dive deeper into Thai culture. It was proof that technology, when deployed with sincerity, could bring people closer together.

Over the years, True Time Thai has grown into a symbol of progress in an authentic environment. They remained true to their roots, right in the energetic streets of Thailand, while shaping the future of communication. It is more than a rental service for translation equipment; it has become a bridge between worlds, built on the foundation of genuine interest in the other and the belief that 'true time' is the time we invest in real connection.

1. Pickup & Return of Equipment

When booking, you select your desired pickup and drop-off location. We currently offer two convenient options in Pattaya:

  • Pattaya Sai Song Road Office: Open from 08:00 to 22:00. Our office is centrally located and easy to reach.
  • Free Hotel Delivery & Drop-off: We deliver the sealed sets for free to the reception of your hotel or resort in Pattaya (including Jomtien and Naklua). You can simply leave the set at reception upon departure.

Note: The rental period starts on the agreed start date from 08:00 and ends on the end date at 22:00 at the latest. Late return without consultation incurs a surcharge of ฿250 per day.
